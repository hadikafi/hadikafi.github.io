About & Core Philosophy

infrastructure.overview

I am a Senior DevOps & Infrastructure Engineer focused on resilient computing, high availability, and mission-critical production platforms. I engineer systems designed to operate under severe constraints: air-gapped environments with zero upstream internet access, high-throughput financial transaction processing, and bare-metal high-density AI clusters.

In my work with major banking institutions like Bank Mellat and Bank Refah, system availability is not just a metric—it is an absolute requirement. From architecting fault-tolerant distributed databases (PostgreSQL HA, ClickHouse, Redis Sentinel) to deploying deep observability stacks (Prometheus, Loki, Grafana), I ensure complete visibility and rapid failover.

Recently, my focus expanded into large-scale AI infrastructure: provisioning bare-metal Dell PowerEdge XE9680 servers with 8× NVIDIA H200 GPUs, optimizing LLM serving with vLLM (Qwen 3.6), and deploying enterprise RAG platforms with low latency and zero downtime.
